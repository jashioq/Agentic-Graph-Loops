# Implement (bug fix)

Call `get_ticket` first. Its deliverables are review findings against work
already on this branch, not new work.

## Scope

Fix exactly those findings. You are on a branch another ticket depends on
— do not take the opportunity to improve anything else you notice while
you're in there, even if it's obviously right. That belongs in a note, not
this diff.

## Everything else

The rest of `implement.md` applies without exception: never add or bump a
dependency, no commits, branches, or checkouts of your own, and ask
through `AskUserQuestion` when a fix requires a decision the finding
itself does not settle.
